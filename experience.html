Enterprise IT

ENSCO

IST Helpdesk Technology Assistant II

February 2024 to July 2025

Vienna, Virginia

Working at ENSCO gave me my most hands on experience in an enterprise IT environment. I supported users both remotely and in person, worked with Tier 1 and Tier 2 issues, helped manage accounts and devices, and gained exposure to areas such as endpoint management, access administration, vulnerability remediation, and technical documentation.

01

User Support

I worked from the Jira help desk queue and helped users troubleshoot hardware, software, connectivity, account, access, and Windows related issues. Depending on the problem, I supported users remotely or worked with them directly onsite.

02

Accounts and Access

I created and configured new user accounts in Active Directory, set up Exchange mailboxes and Webex phone accounts, and worked with department and role based permissions. I also used CyberArk and BeyondTrust when privileged or temporary administrative access was needed.

03

Device Deployment and Management

I helped image and deploy Windows laptops using Microsoft Deployment Toolkit and PowerShell. I also worked with Lansweeper, Ninite, Remote Desktop, Windows drivers and firmware, Surface devices, and asset information while supporting device deployment and maintenance.

04

Vulnerability Remediation

I supported ongoing vulnerability mitigation sprints where outdated software had to be updated or removed from enterprise devices.

One example involved users running outdated versions of LabVIEW that created security vulnerabilities on their machines. I helped remediate affected systems by uninstalling the outdated software through Remote Desktop, Lansweeper, or PowerShell.

When the software could not be removed because of a business or technical requirement, I helped gather the documentation needed for an exception.

05

Documentation

I created internal wiki and technical documentation for software installations, troubleshooting procedures, and recurring IT processes so other members of the team could reference the steps later.

06

Capstone Project

I completed an ENSCO capstone project focused on vulnerability assessment and remediation. The project brought together vulnerability findings, Jira workflows, mitigation processes, and the way IT and cybersecurity teams coordinate remediation efforts.

I presented the project to IT leadership and cybersecurity stakeholders.

What I Learned

ENSCO helped me become much more comfortable working through problems when I did not immediately know the answer. I learned how areas such as user support, identity and access, endpoint management, software deployment, security, and documentation connect in an enterprise environment.

It also helped me understand that IT support is not only about fixing the problem in front of you. Sometimes the solution has to account for security requirements, business needs, user access, documentation, and the effect a change could have on other systems.

Active Directory Exchange Microsoft 365 Outlook Webex Jira Windows MDT PowerShell Lansweeper Ninite Remote Desktop CyberArk BeyondTrust Tenable Windows Update Catalog Technical Documentation
Technology Support

Fairfax County Public Schools

Student Technology Assistant

Summers 2020 and 2021

Springfield, Virginia

My experience with Fairfax County Public Schools was one of my first opportunities to work in a real IT support environment. I helped prepare and support laptops used by students and teachers while also assisting with technology issues during summer school.

What I Worked On

I reimaged and configured laptops so they were running the correct version of Windows 10 and were ready for use.

During summer school, I helped teachers and students troubleshoot technology issues involving laptops, software, hardware, and peripherals.

I also helped prepare devices for distribution before the start of the school year. This included checking laptop inventory, making sure each device ID was correctly matched to the assigned student, and verifying that the laptops were configured and ready for pickup.

What I Learned

FCPS gave me early experience with both sides of IT support: preparing technology behind the scenes and helping people when something was not working.

It also showed me how important organization and asset tracking are when you are managing a large number of devices. A laptop being technically functional is not enough if it is assigned to the wrong student or is not ready when distribution begins.
